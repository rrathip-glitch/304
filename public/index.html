Sri Lankan trick-taking for two teams of two.

v1 solo · rules

How to play 304

  1. Deal: 8 cards each, 4 players, partners opposite. You (bottom) partner with top.
  2. Bid: promise a number of points your team will win in tricks. Minimum 160. 190 is rare.
  3. Trump: highest bidder picks the trump suit (hidden at first).
  4. Play: counter-clockwise. Must follow suit. If you can't, your card is played face-down.
  5. Ranks (high→low): J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8, 7.
  6. Points: J=3, 9=2, A=1.1, 10=1, K=0.3, Q=0.2, 8=7=0. Total = 30.4.
  7. Win tokens: meet your bid → win tokens; fail → lose tokens. All 8 tricks = 5 tokens (high court).
  8. Match: each team starts with 11 tokens; first to 22 wins.
